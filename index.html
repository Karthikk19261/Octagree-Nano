Eco-Friendly Coatings

Why low-VOC matters for your family

Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) are the chemicals you smell in fresh paint — and they linger long after the walls dry. They contribute to indoor air pollution, ozone formation and respiratory issues.

Octagreen paints are water-based, low-VOC and odour-light. Safe for nurseries, hospitals and offices. No more “new paint headache.” Just clean walls, clean air.

  • ✓ Lead-free, mercury-free formulation
  • ✓ Anti-fungal, anti-algae, washable surfaces
  • ✓ Quick-drying — repaint within 4 hours
  • ✓ Tintable to thousands of custom colours
